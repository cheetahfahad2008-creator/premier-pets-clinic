Why Premier Pets

Medicine with heart, science with soul

Founded in 2011, Premier Pets Clinic began with one simple belief: every animal deserves the same standard of care we'd want for ourselves. Today our fear-free, AAHA-accredited clinic blends cutting-edge diagnostics with good old-fashioned kindness.

  • ✓ Fear-Free certified handling for anxious pets
  • ✓ In-house lab, ultrasound & digital X-ray
  • ✓ Transparent pricing — estimates before treatment
  • ✓ Separate cat & dog waiting areas
