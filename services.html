Common questions

Before you visit

What happens at a first visit?
Your vet examines your pet nose to tail, listens to your concerns, and explains everything in plain language. You approve a written estimate before any treatment. Plan for 30–45 minutes — first visits are unhurried on purpose.
How do I pay, and when do I know the cost?
You’ll see a written estimate before we begin — you approve it first, always. We accept cards and cash at the front desk after the visit.
What vaccination schedule does my pet need?
Puppies and kittens start their core vaccines around 6–8 weeks, with boosters every 3–4 weeks until about 16 weeks. Adults need boosters yearly or every three years depending on the vaccine. We map out the schedule for you and send reminders.
Do you treat birds, rabbits or other exotic pets?
Yes — our dentistry and exotics vet sees birds, rabbits and most small companion animals. Mention the species when you book so we schedule the right doctor and enough time.
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are welcome every open day, Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Booking ahead means little to no waiting — and in an emergency, call 3001 1105 so we’re ready when you arrive.

Not sure which service your pet needs?

Book a wellness exam and we’ll take it from there — or call us and describe what you’re seeing.
