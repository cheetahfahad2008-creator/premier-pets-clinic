About & team

A clinic built on precision and patience

Who we are, how we work, and the people your pet will actually meet.

Premier Pets Clinic began with one belief: every animal deserves the same standard of care we would want for ourselves.

Today our Doha clinic pairs modern diagnostics — in-house lab, ultrasound and digital X-ray — with something rarer: time. Appointments are unhurried, waiting areas are separate for cats and dogs, and nothing happens to your pet before you’ve seen and approved a written estimate.

We serve pet families in English and Arabic, six days a week, from morning until 10 at night — because worry doesn’t keep office hours. نتحدث العربية
