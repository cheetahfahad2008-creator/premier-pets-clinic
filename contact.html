Your first visit

Three steps, no stress

First time at a vet — or first time at ours? Here’s exactly what to expect.

Bring what you have

Any vaccination records or past reports, and the carrier or leash your pet already trusts. No paperwork? That’s fine — we’ll start a file.

The unhurried exam

Your vet examines your pet nose to tail, explains what they find in plain language, and answers every question — then you approve a written estimate before anything else happens.

Leave with a plan

Most first visits take 30–45 minutes. You leave knowing what’s next, what it costs, and when we’d like to see your pet again — if at all.
